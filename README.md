# Bloom
**A little space to pause, reflect, and grow.**

## About Bloom

Bloom is a wellness and productivity app designed for young women aged 15–30. Life can get busy, and sometimes we forget to take care of ourselves while trying to get everything done.

Bloom helps users organize their tasks, express their thoughts through journaling, and keep track of their daily wellness. It's a space to slow down, reflect, and take things one day at a time.

## Features

### Tasks
- Create and manage daily tasks.
- Tick off tasks as you complete them.
- Keep track of your daily progress.

### Daily Journal
- Write about your thoughts and feelings.
- Get daily prompts for inspiration.
- Record things you're grateful for.
- Rate your day and reflect on your mood.
- Upload photos to save memories.
- Lock journal entries for privacy.

### Wellness
- View your journal entries and daily records in a calendar.
- Use a breathing timer to relax.
- Track your daily water intake.
- Record your sleep hours, sleep quality, and dreams.
- Read a daily affirmation.

### Account
- Log in to your personal account.
- Save your information using Firebase.

## How to Use Bloom

**Tasks:** Set your goals in the morning and tick them off as you finish them. At the end of the day, take a moment to look back at what you've done.

**Daily Journal:** Write freely, answer a prompt, or list things you're grateful for. You can also rate your day, upload photos, and lock your entries.

**Wellness:** Track your water intake and sleep, use the breathing timer when you need a break, and check your calendar to look back on your progress.
